How the heist works

  1. Loose the arrow. Your bet goes into the contract and the arrow starts flying. The loot multiplier follows a fixed curve of time: 2× after about 2.3 seconds, 10× after 6.4, up to 1000×.
  2. Tap RUN. The contract checks the flight time on the chain (you can never claim more than the arrow reached) and only then NEAR rolls the Sheriff’s dice: the point where he snaps the arrow.
  3. Escape or get caught. If the Sheriff’s point is at or past yours, you are paid bet × your multiplier on the spot. If not, half of your bet is burned for good and half stays in the vault. Wait more than 20 seconds and the Sheriff wins.

The odds, in the open

Run at any multiplier x and your chance is 97% ÷ x: 48.5% at 2×, 9.7% at 10×. Because the dice is rolled when you run, waiting changes how much you win and how likely you are to win it, never the fairness. The Sheriff keeps a 3% edge, so the vault never runs dry, and one heist can never win more than 1% of the vault. Every heist is an on-chain event anyone can check.

Why it matters for the coin

Every failed heist burns coins forever, so the more the Guild plays, the scarcer the coin gets. The other half refills the vault that pays the winners.
